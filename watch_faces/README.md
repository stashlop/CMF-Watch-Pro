# Omnitrix watch faces: CMF Watch Pro 1 (D395)

Install-ready static faces for the **custom photo face** in the official CMF Watch app.
They're generated by `python -m omnitrix.watch_release --all`; don't edit the PNGs by hand.

| File | Theme |
|---|---|
| `omnitrix_home.png` | Omnitrix (green) |
| `heatblast.png` … `ripjaws.png` | One per alien (10, including `stinkfly.png`) |
| `previews/` | Mock-ups with the app clock at the top or the bottom (not for installing) |

Every face is 410 × 502 (spec-sheet resolution, not yet confirmed on the watch). The dial is centred, and
the top (y < 119) and bottom (y > 383) bands are kept black for the watch's own clock.

## Installing (done by you, in the official app)

1. Copy a PNG to your phone (USB cable, cloud drive, or email it to yourself).
2. In the CMF Watch app, open the watch-face section and choose the custom/photo face option.
   Menu names may differ in your app version.
3. Pick the PNG. If the app offers a crop, keep the **whole** image.
4. Put the clock at the **top** or the **bottom**, not the centre, so it doesn't cover the dial.
5. Sync to the watch.

If the edges look cut off or stretched, install `data/exports/omnitrix/phone/calibration.png` the same
way and report what you see. The faces will be re-exported at the real size.

Nothing in this project sends data to the watch. Installation goes only through the official app.
